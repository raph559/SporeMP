## Roles and admission

`replica_policy` is an engine-independent policy/registry, not a simulator. A process has one immutable role: single player, authority, or replica. Only authority may publish outcomes. Replica application admits absolute state inside a scoped guard while continuing to deny gameplay/reward callbacks. A failed, reentrant or invalidated application quarantines its baseline; it cannot retry an uncertain native side effect automatically.

The registry holds at most 32 records. Identity is `(source worker generation, source scene, remote entity ID, entity incarnation)` plus a monotonically increasing local baseline token. Each entity's sample sequence must increase. Native pointers never cross IPC or artifact boundaries. Binding adopts an already resolved local diagnostic ID and creates no noun. Destruction tombstones the binding before the native destructor; scene exit/disconnect invalidates it. Tombstones cannot be reused until a fresh baseline. Stale generation/scene, destruction/reuse and callback invalidation are HOST-tested. Native evidence covers exact existing-noun adoption, duplicate sequence, disconnect and fresh-baseline admission. Reliable network spawn/despawn and authenticated reconnect remain M06 work.

The current adapter resolves only the living main Creature avatar through the live native census. It refuses pending owned actions or a second native player context. It copies health/energy/hunger fields and calls the pure DNA setter. Health must be positive and no greater than the native effective maximum returned through the adjusted Combatant receiver. That maximum is 10 in this fixture; the raw field named `mMaxHealthPoints` is 1. Values must be finite and bounded. Death/max-health changes, B reward accounts, full inventories, positions and full progression are outside this scalar adapter.
