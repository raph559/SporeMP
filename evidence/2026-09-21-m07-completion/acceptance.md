## Interpretation of original outcomes

The paired target-choice design was declared before native19. Both owners must actually damage the same NPC incarnation in each trial; the NPC retaliates against A in the normal trial and B in the complementary impaired trial. These are separate fresh encounters with the same pinned fixture/species/archetype. Reused numeric IDs do not imply the same incarnation across sessions. Native18's stricter same-fight target-switch driver remains inconclusive; this decision does not relabel it as a successful switch.

The resource contest is original first feeding and nutrition, not an invented positive DNA pickup. The winning first-feed callback returns the game's configured zero DNA; subsequent original callbacks transfer food to hunger/health. Exact source revisions establish both native client projections. Once the original pool return retires the consumed incarnation, acceptance requires the ordered retirement/despawn/client-removal chain and unchanged owner balances. Later reuse with a new generation does not resurrect the consumed resource.

Native27's operator inspects all three original starting worlds, confirms the actual original victory dialog, inspects both clients' rounded green corpse views, and inspects fresh B after Connected with displayed health8 and DNA7. Native28's fresh three-world contact sheet is also inspected before its movement/jump sequence. Numerical motion and landing observations come from the original native traces, not from static images. The selected stills do not qualify every animation frame, audio quality or frame timing.

The packet filter is scoped to the dedicated loopback TCP session, with uniform ±5ms jitter around25ms delay per captured packet and1% Bernoulli loss using seed731. TLS remains real.
